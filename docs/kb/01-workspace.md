# Workspace and invitations

## Create a workspace
To create a workspace, choose New workspace from the workspace menu, enter a name, and select Create. The creator becomes the workspace Owner. Each account can create up to three workspaces in this fictional demo product.

## Workspace invitations
Workspace Owners and Admins can invite members from Settings > Members > Invite. Invitation links expire after 7 days. To replace an expired invitation link, an Owner or Admin must open Settings > Members and choose Resend invite.

## Member roles
Owners can manage billing, remove a workspace, and assign member roles. Admins can invite members and manage projects but cannot change billing or remove the workspace. Viewers can read shared projects but cannot invite members, edit projects, or change billing.
