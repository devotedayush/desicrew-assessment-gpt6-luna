# Notifications and help

## Notification preferences
Each member can change email notifications from Account > Notifications. Project mentions and assigned tasks have separate email switches. Muting a project's activity emails does not disable direct mention notifications.

## Support hours
The fictional MeadowDesk support hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. Send support questions to help@meadowdesk.example. These hours describe the demo policy and do not promise a live response time.
