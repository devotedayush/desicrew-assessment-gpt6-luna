# Plans and billing

## Trial period
A new MeadowDesk workspace starts with a 14-day Pro trial. No payment card is required for the trial. When the trial ends, the workspace changes to the Free plan unless the Owner has chosen a paid plan.

## Cancel a subscription
Only the workspace Owner can cancel a paid subscription from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. After cancellation takes effect, the workspace changes to the Free plan and existing project data remains available within the Free plan limits.

## Refund policy
The fictional MeadowDesk policy allows a refund request within 7 calendar days of the first paid subscription charge. Renewal charges are not eligible for refunds under this demo policy. To request an eligible refund, the Owner must email help@meadowdesk.example with the workspace name and invoice number.

## Seats and invoices
Paid plans charge for active Owners, Admins, and Editors; Viewers do not use paid seats. An Owner can download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Removing a paid member reduces the seat count on the next renewal; the current billing period is not prorated.
